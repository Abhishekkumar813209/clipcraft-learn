PATTERN 01

Circular — sab centre ki taraf (basic)

Trigger: “…sitting around a circular table, all facing towards the centre.” Koi direction-variable nahi. Yahi sabse simple base hai — left = clockwise, right = anti-clockwise.

Ex 1–5 (8 persons E–L): Start un clue se jo 100% fix ho → “L, E ke immediate right” + “I, E se 3rd left” → 2 cases.
“Only two persons between I and H, who sits immediate left of J” → dono case fill.
Final killer clue: “G immediate right of K” → Case-2 eliminate.
Mini-rule: facing centre → uska left tumhare screen par clockwise. Har baar bottom seat se bharna shuru karo.
✓

PATTERN 02

Circular — kuch andar, kuch bahar mukh

Trigger: “Three of them are facing the centre while others are facing outside.” Ab left/right ulta ho jaata hai bahar-mukhi log ke liye.

Ex 6–10 (P–W, 8 log): Pehle direction-anchor lo: “R faces centre. P 2nd to right of R. W (outside) opposite P.”
Phir “S immediately left of V (outside)” → 2 cases.
“U 2nd right of T, both same direction” + “neither U nor T adjacent to S” → filter.
Last: “Q not adjacent to P, both same direction” → Case 1 out.
Mini-rule: ‘opposite’ sirf even number of persons pe hi 100% kaam karta hai (8 log → gap 4).
✓

PATTERN 03

Circular + Days/Months variable

Trigger: circle ke saath “different days Monday to Sunday / different months”. Yahan do cheezein ek saath baithani hai — seat + day.

Ex 11–15 (7 girls A–G): “G sits on Saturday immediately after B and before A” → B=Friday, A=Sunday (day-order = seat-order dono lock).
“One girl between D and G, four between A and D” → 2 cases → “Three girls between C and G”, “D/G Tuesday wale ke adjacent nahi” se cut.
“F Wednesday, 2nd right of A” → Case 2 out. Final: A(Sun) C(Thu) D(Mon) B(Fri) G(Sat) F(Wed) E(Tue).
Mini-rule: day-based circle mein ‘immediately after’ = agla din AND agli seat — dono ek saath maan lo tabhi jaldi bhesta hai.

PATTERN 04

Circular + Age/Number variable (older/younger)

Trigger: “different ages 24, 16, 19, 30…”, “X is 12 years older than Y”. Numbers hi asli filter hain, seating nahi.

Ex 16–20 (A,B,C,M,N,O,V,W): “C is 12 years older than A” → given list mein sirf 24 & 36 ka hi 12 ka gap banta hai → age lock, phir seat.
“Age difference between immediate neighbours of W is 6” → 24 & 30.
“N is 9 years older than M” → 19 & 10.
Mini-rule: pehle given numbers ke saare valid pairs (difference wale) likh lo — usually ek hi possible hota hai, wahi entry point hai.

PATTERN 05

Circular + Blood Relation (family tree)

Trigger: “H’s husband…”, “D’s daughter…”, “A is the father of G”. Do diagram banao — ek circle, ek family tree — warna gadbad pakki.

Ex 21–25 (A–H): “F 2nd left of D’s daughter”, “only one person between F and A”, “A is father of G” → skeleton.
“H’s husband 2nd right of B” + “no female immediate side of B” → 2 cases → “E aur H’s mother ke beech ek person” → Case 2 mein E ki jagah hi nahi → rejected.
Answers: A = H ka husband, H = G ki mother, H = D ki sister.
Mini-rule: ‘X’s husband/mother’ jaise phrase = seat clue + relation clue dono. Gender ko ⊕/⊖ mark karke circle pe likho.

PATTERN 06

Circular — Uncertain number of persons

Trigger: “Certain number of persons… more than 15 are sitting.” Total pata nahi — isliye ek reference person se gaps count karke total nikalna hota hai.

Ex 26–30: “Four persons between M and N”, “O 3rd left of M”, “two between O and P”, “Q exactly between M and P”.
Balancing clue: “N–Q (right of Q) = M–O (left of O)” → equation ban gaya.
“More than 15” condition se chhota case cut → Total = 17.
Mini-rule: uncertain circle = ‘…counted from left/right of X’ wale clue hi total decide karte hain. Dono taraf ka count likho: n = a + b + 2.

PATTERN 07

Linear single row — sab ek hi direction

Trigger: “sitting in a row, facing towards the North.” Simple: North = tumhara left = West (row ka left end).

Ex 31–35 (A–H, 8 seats): Anchor = “A at extreme end, third to the left of H” → A ki 2 possible ends → 2 cases.
“B adjacent to H, 2nd left of E” → dono case fill.
“D immediately left of F”, “F/G kabhi end pe nahi” → tight.
“G not adjacent to A” → Case 2 out.

PATTERN 08

Linear single row — kuch North, kuch South

Trigger: “Four of them facing North while others facing South.” Har person ka arrow lagana zaroori — South-facing ka left/right ulta.

PATTERN 09

Double row (parallel) — aamne-saamne baithna

Trigger: “two parallel rows… row-1 facing South, row-2 facing North, each person faces the other.” Row-1 ka left = Row-2 ka right.

Ex 41–45 (D–O, 12 log): “F 4th to the left of H” → row-1 mein sirf 2 slots → “L immediate to the person who faces F” + “two persons between L and O” → 3 cases.
“M immediate to O, facing E” + “I immediate to E” → aur cases.
“N not immediate to L”, “H does not face N”, “G not at end”, “K neither faces F nor I” → case 1 & 3 out; “J and M not immediate” → final.
Mini-rule: ‘faces’ wala clue seedha vertical link hai — pehle vertical pairs fix karo, phir horizontal.

PATTERN 19

Clue-grammar: AND / BUT / WHILE vs WHO / WHOM / WHOSE

Trigger: ek hi vakya mein do position di ho. Yahi sabse zyada silly mistake karata hai.

AND-case → pehle naam par lagega.
“D is 3rd to the left of A and 2nd to the right of F” → D–A ke beech 2, aur D–F ke beech 1.
WHO-case → doosre naam par lagega.
“D is 3rd to the left of A who is 2nd to the left of H” → D–A ke beech 2, aur A–H ke beech 1.
Rule: AND = subject wahi rehta, WHO = subject badal jaata hai.
✓

PATTERN 20

Clue-grammar: Between / Exactly between / Immediate neighbour

Trigger: “G is between A and H”, “exactly between”, “immediate neighbour”, “not adjacent”. Har ek ka alag matlab hai.

Between → G ke dono taraf A aur H (order fixed nahi) → possible info, 2 cases.
Exactly between → A aur H ke beech odd number of persons, G bilkul beech mein.
Immediate neighbour of both → ye between ka hi case hai.
Not adjacent / neither… nor → negative info: koi seat fix nahi karta, sirf cases kaatta hai — isliye ise hamesha last mein lagao.
✓

PATTERN 21

Clue ko priority do: Definite → Comparative → Possible → Negative

Trigger: har set mein 6–8 clue hote hain. Jo student upar se neeche padh ke lagata hai wo phasta hai. Order badlo.

1. Definite: “A sits at the extreme left end”, “F sits on Wednesday” → seat pakki.
2. Comparative: “A is 4th to the left of B” → do log ka relative block.
3. Possible: “A sits between B and C” → 2 cases banao.
4. Negative: “A is not adjacent to B”, “neither X nor Y” → ye sirf case eliminate karte hain. Inhe pehle lagaoge to time waste hoga.
✓

PATTERN 22

Q-type: “What is the position of X with respect to Y?”

Trigger: sabse common question. Y base hai, X ko count karna hai — ulta karoge to answer galat.

Ex 4: “Position of F w.r.t. J” → J se ginn ke F = immediate right.
Ex 9: W w.r.t. V → 3rd to the left.
Circle mein trap: ek hi answer do tarah se sahi ho sakta hai (Ex 13: B is 3rd to the left and 4th to the right of A) → tab option “Both (1) and (3)” hota hai. Circle mein hamesha dono side check karo: left-count + right-count = total − 1.
