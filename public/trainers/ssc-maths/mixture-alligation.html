PATTERN 01

"Kitna bhaag replace karein" → ratio se fraction

PATTERN 02

Replacement jab data % mein ho (acid/water solution)

Trigger: "solution mein water x% tha, N litre nikal ke utna hi paani daala, ab acid y% hai — shuru mein kitna paani/solution tha?"

Q23: Acid 36% → 30%. 36/100 × R = 30/100 → R = 5/6 (left/original) → nikala 1/6 = 4L → 1 unit = 4 → O.Q = 24 L
Water = 24 × 64/100 = 15.36 L

PATTERN 03

Fixed litre replace → initial quantity nikalo

Trigger: "N litre mixture nikaal ke utne hi litre liquid B daala, ratio a:b ho gaya — shuru mein A kitna tha?"

Q24: A:B 4:1 → 2:3, A ka fraction: 4/5 × R = 2/5 → R = 1/2 → nikla 1/2 = 10L → 1 unit = 10, O.Q = 20 → A = 4/5 × 20 = 16 L Q25: 7:5 → 7:9, 9L replace: 7/12 × R = 7/16 → R = 3/4 → 1/4 = 9L → O.Q = 36 → A = 7/12 × 36 = 21 L
