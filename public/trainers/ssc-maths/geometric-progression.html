PATTERN 01

Series di hai → koi bhi term (a·rⁿ⁻¹)

Trigger: "6, 12, 24, … ka 11th term?" ya "a=216, r=5/6 → 4th term?" — a aur r saamne hain, seedha power lagao.

Q2: 6,12,24 → r=2 → a₁₁ = 6×2¹⁰ = 6144 Q: 4,12,36 → r=3 → a₆ = 4×3⁵ = 972

PATTERN 03

"Kaunsa term hai / kitne terms hain?" → base same karke power match

Trigger: "GP 6, 18, 54, …, 39366 mein kitne terms hain?" / "2, 1, ½, … ka kaunsa term 1/128 hai?"

Q3: 6·3ⁿ⁻¹=39366 → 3ⁿ⁻¹=6561=3⁸ → n = 9 Q1: 2(½)ⁿ⁻¹=1/128 → (½)ⁿ⁻¹=(½)⁸ → n = 9

PATTERN 04

Do term diye → DIVIDE karo (AP mein subtract, GP mein bhaag)

Trigger: "2nd term 24 aur 5th term 81 hai, GP nikaalo" — bade term ko chhote se divide karo, r^(farq) mil jayega.

Ex: ar⁴/ar = 81/24 → r³=27/8 → r=3/2 → a=16
GP = 16, 24, 36, 54…

PATTERN 06

r = 2±√3; increasing hai isliye r = 2+√3

PATTERN 07

PATTERN 09

Finite sum: Sn = a(rⁿ−1)/(r−1)

Trigger: "3 + 3² + … + 3⁸ ka sum" ya "2/5 + 2/25 + … n terms tak" — r>1 par (rⁿ−1)/(r−1), r<1 par (1−rⁿ)/(1−r).

Q9: 3(3⁸−1)/2 = 3×3280 = 9840 Q10: a=2/5, r=1/5 → (2/5)(1−(1/5)ⁿ)/(4/5) = ½[1−(1/5)ⁿ]

PATTERN 11

PATTERN 16

AM–GM–HM: GM² = AM × HM

Trigger: "do numbers ka GM 8 aur HM 6.4 hai" / "AM aur GM diya hai toh x³+y³?" — AM=(a+b)/2, GM=√(ab), HM=2ab/(a+b); sum aur product nikaalte hi kaam khatam.

Q22: ab=64, a+b=2×64/6.4=20 → numbers = 4, 16 Q23: AM = GM²/HM = 144/10 = 14.4
Q24: sum S, product P → HM = 2P/S
