PATTERN 01

Present ratio + "n saal baad" ratio → unit nikaalo

Trigger: "vartaman aayu ka anupat a:b hai, n varsh baad anupat c:d ho jayega" — dono ratio ko cross-multiply karke 1 unit = kitne saal.

Q2: M:D = 7:1, 5 yr baad 4:1 → 7x+5=4(x+5) → x=5 → diff 6x = 30 yrs Q5: A:B=3:5, 5 yr baad 13:20 → 1 unit=7y → B=5×7 = 35 yrs
Q14: F:S=15:8, 6 yr pehle 13:6 → 1 unit=3y → F=15×3 = 45 yrs

PATTERN 06

Umr ka ANTAR fixed diya ho

Trigger: "pita aur putra ki aayu me 15 varsh ka antar hai" / "X, Y se 6 varsh chhota hai" — difference kabhi nahi badalta, wahi 1 unit ka base hai.

Q4: diff=15; 5y pehle F=2S → S−5=15 → S=20, F=35 → 35 & 20 yrs Q12: S:P=2:3, diff 1 unit=6y → 12,18 → 6y baad = 3:4

PATTERN 07

Aayu ka YOG diya ho (sum = fixed)

Trigger: "unki vartaman aayu ka yog 44/52 varsh hai" — ratio ho toh total units baant do, warna S = Sum − F rakh ke equation.

Q13: 3:8, sum=44 → 11 unit=44 → 1 unit=4 → diff 5 unit = 20 yrs Q23: F+S=52; 4y baad S=¼F → F=44,S=8 → 10y baad 18:54 = 1:3

PATTERN 08

Average wale age question (naya banda join)

Trigger: "x varsh pehle teen logo ki aayu ka ausat A tha, ab ek aur judne par chaaro ka ausat B hai" — pehle sabki age ko present pe laao (+x each), phir total ka antar.

Q15: 6y pehle avg 32 → ab avg 38, sum=114; 4 logo ka sum=144 → S = 30 yrs
Shortcut: New = Old ± change×members = 38 − 2×4 = 30 Q11: m:S=5:3, avg 32 → sum 64 → 8 unit=64 → 40,24 → 6y baad = 23:15
