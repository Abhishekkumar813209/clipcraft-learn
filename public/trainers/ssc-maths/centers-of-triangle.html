PATTERN 01

Internal bisector → AB/AC = BD/DC

Trigger: "AD is the internal bisector of ∠A meeting BC at D" / "XQ bisects ∠X" — turant sides ka ratio = base ke tukdon ka ratio.

Q1: BD=5, BC=7.5 → DC=2.5; AB:AC = 5:2.5 = 2 : 1 Q2: XY=5, XZ=7 → YQ:QZ = 5 : 7

PATTERN 02

DC = k·BD diya ho + sides ka ANTAR diya ho

Trigger: "DC = 2BD" aur "AC − AB = 5 cm" — ratio se AB=1u, AC=2u; antar = 1u.

Q3: AB:AC = BD:DC = 1:2 → AC−AB = 1u = 5 → AB = 5 cm

PATTERN 03

D ki doori BC ke MID-POINT se

Trigger: "distance of D from the mid-point of BC" — pehle BD nikalo ratio se, phir |BD − BC/2|.

Q4: AB:AC = 5:7.5 = 2:3 → BD = 10×2/5 = 4; mid = 5 → DM = 1 cm

PATTERN 04

Median + ∠ADB, ∠ADC ke bisectors → EF ∥ BC

Trigger: "AD is a median, bisectors of ∠ADB and ∠ADC meet AB and AC at E and F" — EF ∥ BC, so EF/BC = AE/AB.

Q5: AE:BE = 3:4 → AE:AB = 3:7 → EF : BC = 3 : 7

PATTERN 05

Trigger: "∠A = 90°, AD bisector, DE ⊥ AC" — BD:DC = AB:AC, phir DE/AB = DC/BC.

Q6: AB:AC = 10:15 = 2:3 → DE/AB = 3/5 → DE = 10×3/5 = 6 cm

PATTERN 06

PATTERN 07

PATTERN 17
