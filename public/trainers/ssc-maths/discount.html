A · BASE + BUY-GET-FREE (P1–P4)

✓

PATTERN 01

SRC · p4 (definitions) · p5–p6 (ratio method)

Base formula — D% hamesha MP par

Trigger: "marked price / अंकित मूल्य ... discount ... selling price" — koi bhi discount question.

Formula: D = MP − SP, D% = (MP−SP)/MP × 100. Discount CP par kabhi nahi lagta. Ratio trick (p5): MP:SP = 4:3 → D% = 1/4 × 100 = 25%. Ratio dikhte hi difference/MP kar do.

Q1 (p7): Buy 7 get 2 free → 2/9 × 100 = 22.22% (14.28% trap = 1/7, galat). Q2 (p8): Buy 3 get 2 free → 2/5 = 40%.  Q3 (p9): 24 par 6 free → 6/30 = 20%.
