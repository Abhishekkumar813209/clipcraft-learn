PATTERN 01

Do vessels ka mixture given ratio mein mila diya → naya ratio?

Trigger: "do bartan mein milk:water 1:4 aur 3:8, dono ko 3:2 mein mila diya → naye mixture ka ratio?" — mixing ratio DIYA hai, answer nikalna hai.

Q1: Milk fraction 1/5 & 3/11, mix 3:2 → milk=(1/5×3 + 3/11×2)/5 = 63/275 → water=212/275 → 63 : 212

Q6: Acid 10% & 30%, target 25% → 5:15 = 1 : 3
